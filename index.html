The idea

Your life, built like a house.

Most planners hand you a list that never ends. Life House gives you a home that grows with you. You start with one small room and a few tiny steps. As you get things done, rooms light up, fill with furniture, and new ones appear.

Every part of your life is a roomWork, family, running, the guitar you keep meaning to pick up. Each one gets a room of its own.
Lights show where your energy wentFinish something and that room lights up. Leave it for a while and it dims. No red badges, no streak to break. One small task brings the light back.
Furniture is proof of progressEvery finished task opens a chest with something for that room. Months later, you can look around and see what you did.

How to start

Small on purpose.

Big plans are where most of us get stuck. So Life House starts tiny, and grows only as fast as you do.

1

Answer two questions

Your name, and what has to happen this week. That's the whole setup.

Type it, or just talk. The AI turns whatever you say into a first room and a few small to-dos.

2

Get one room and three choices

Your house starts as a single, dim room. Life House picks three small to-dos to choose from: a quick one, one that's due soon, and one that moves you forward.

You pick where to start. The app never shows you the whole pile.

3

Finish things, open chests

Every to-do you finish opens a treasure chest. Inside: furniture for that room, from common to epic. Doing stuff earns you something you can see.

Stuck on a big one? Tap Break it down and get steps that take minutes. Every step gets its own chest.

4

Grow into the life you want

Once you have some momentum, Life House asks one gentle question at a time. Where do you want to be in a year? A hobby you miss? How are you sleeping?

Your answer becomes a new room with easy first steps, aimed at where you want to go.
